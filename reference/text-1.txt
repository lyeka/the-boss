Do you really want to be the Boss? Yes, you do -- so that you can close mega deals and amass a fortune! You will start 
this game as the Leader of a specialist Investor Team who wants to participate in lots of deals. Whenever possible, 
you also want to be the Boss who controls and closes those deals. But of course all your fellow Investors have the 
same goal. You will need to be flexible, and willing to change specialties when necessary. Negotiate and insinuate 
your way into as many deals as possible. Take charge and Be The Boss!

Game Design: Sid Sackson
The Board
The gameboard shows 16 potential business deals. Each 
of these deals shows a number of dividends to be paid out, 
and the specific investors needed to make the deal. The more 
investors needed for a deal, the higher the dividends paid. 
The value of each dividend is determined at the time the deal 
is made during the game.
